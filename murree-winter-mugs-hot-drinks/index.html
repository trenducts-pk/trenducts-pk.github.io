Hot Drinks for the Murree Winter: Choosing the Right Mug for Chai, Coffee and Soup

From Murree and Nathia Gali to cold Islamabad evenings, a good mug makes winter better. Here is how to choose mugs that keep drinks warm and look good doing it.

Winter in the hills of Pakistan has its own rhythm. In Murree, Nathia Gali and Ayubia, mornings start below freezing and fog rolls through the pine trees. Even in Islamabad and Peshawar, December nights are cold enough that everyone wants something hot in their hands. Doodh patti, Kashmiri chai, instant coffee, hot chocolate for the kids, or a bowl of chicken corn soup from the stall near Mall Road: the drinks change, but the habit is the same.

A good mug sounds like a small thing, but it affects how long your drink stays warm, how comfortable it is to hold, and how much you enjoy it. This guide explains what to look for.

Step 01

Material matters more than looks

Different materials behave differently in the cold:

  • Ceramic and stoneware hold heat well and feel solid and warm in the hand
  • Thick glass looks beautiful with coloured drinks but loses heat faster
  • Double-wall glass keeps drinks warm while staying cool to touch
  • Stainless steel insulated mugs keep drinks hot the longest, ideal for travel

For sitting by the heater at home, a thick ceramic mug is hard to beat. If you are heading out for a walk on the Mall or a drive to Patriata, an insulated option makes more sense.

Step 02

Size and shape for different drinks

Pakistani chai is often served in small cups, but in winter people tend to want more. A 300 to 350 ml mug suits a generous chai or coffee. Wider mugs cool down faster, so they suit people who like to sip quickly, while taller, narrower mugs keep heat in longer. For soup, a mug with a wide mouth and a big handle is easiest. Children do better with smaller, lighter mugs they can hold safely with both hands.

Step 03

Pre-warm your mug

This simple trick keeps drinks hot for much longer. Before pouring chai, fill the mug with hot water from the kettle for thirty seconds, then empty it. A cold ceramic mug in a Murree hotel room can steal a lot of heat from your drink in the first minute. Pre-warming solves that instantly.

Step 04

Mugs as winter gifts

Mugs are one of the most popular winter gifts in Pakistan, for good reason. They are affordable, personal and used every day. Couple mug sets with matching spoons suit newlyweds and anniversaries. Textured charcoal or matte mugs suit people who like a minimal look. Glass mugs with fun shapes appeal to students and teenagers. You can look through ceramic and glass mugs for chai and coffee to find one that matches the person.

Step 05

Caring for your mugs

Tea leaves stains, especially in light ceramic. Clean stains with a paste of baking soda and water, rubbed gently with a soft sponge. Avoid pouring boiling water into a very cold glass mug, since a sudden temperature change can crack it. Let mugs cool before putting them in water. Store them hanging on hooks or upside down on a shelf so dust does not collect inside.

Step 06

Creating a winter drinks corner

If your family drinks several cups a day in winter, a small drinks corner saves time. Place the kettle, a jar of tea leaves, sugar, a few sachets of coffee and hot chocolate, and four or five mugs on a tray near a power point. Add a small spoon holder and a cloth. Guests can help themselves, and the kitchen stays tidy.

Step 07

Visiting the hills

If you are travelling to Murree or the northern areas in winter, pack a flask and one insulated mug per person. Roadside chai is part of the fun, but in snowfall traffic jams you may be stuck for hours. A hot drink from your own flask can make a long wait much more bearable. Check weather and road conditions before travelling, as roads can close during heavy snow.

Step 08

Final thoughts

A mug is a daily companion all winter long. Choose the material based on where you drink, pick a size that suits your habits, pre-warm it, and take care of it. Whether you are watching snowfall in Murree or sitting near the heater in Islamabad, the right mug makes every cup of chai a little more comforting.
